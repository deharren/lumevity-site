How the score works. Each image is measured on four things that separate a flat vector atlas plate from a photograph: how much of it is clean white ground, how much of it is flat unbroken color, how small its palette is, and how little photographic grain it carries. Higher is more on brand. It is a first pass to sort the queue, not a verdict, so trust your eye over the number.
